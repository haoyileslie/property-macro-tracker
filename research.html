Research Interpretation

The published indexes use equal weights as a transparent baseline; alternative weights belong in robustness testing.

  • Equal weighting avoids choosing weights with the same housing outcome later used for evaluation.
  • Within-block averaging stops a block with many correlated rate series from dominating by variable count.
  • PCA and dynamic-factor versions can test common-factor robustness; VAR or supervised weights can test target-specific predictability.
  • Any estimated weighting scheme must be re-estimated inside each historical training window to prevent leakage.
  • Use the sign, percentile, persistence and block contribution together rather than reading one monthly move in isolation.
